### Business Scenario

Training departments need customized learning plans for employees based on their individual needs and career objectives.

### Solution

Build an **AI-powered Training Recommendation System** that can:

* Recommend relevant training programs based on **skills, learning history, and career goals**
* Identify potential **skill gaps**
* Generate personalized **learning paths**
* Use an **AI Agent** to interact with users and assist with training recommendations
* Provide explanations for recommendations

### Expected Results

* Improved course engagement and completion
* Personalized learning experience
* Better alignment between training and career goals
* Improved visibility into employee skill development

### Deliverables

1. **Recommendation Engine**
2. **User Profiling Module**
3. **AI Training Agent**
4. **Dashboard**
5. **Analytics Report**
